# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 29: Reika's Choice

Reika summoned every remaining Beta follower.

“I do not ask you to choose Raizen’s blood. Choose the future you want after him.”

Kaien, Hagan, and Moro stood between loyalties. They still honored Gorzan. They had also felt his chains.

Gorzan appeared through projected fire, naming Reika a child deceived by humans.

Reika did not answer with rage.

“Gorzan raised me. He protected our people when I could not. I will not erase that service.” Her voice trembled, then steadied. “But love is not permission to coerce. Debt is not a chain. My father’s death is not a throne for our fear.”

Kaien extinguished the projection.

Hagan and Moro planted their weapons before Reika—not surrender, but a promise to stand.

The Beta Sigil brightened, yet remained incomplete. The orcs had not recognized her. Reika did not send anyone to force them.

“I will oppose Gorzan,” she told Luca. “But I will not kill him if he can still be saved.”

“I do not want that either.”

“Our wishes may not decide the ending,” Serena said.

Deep below, Vhargos opened eyes made of magma.

**End of Chapter 29**
