# Sovereign of Aethra

## Epilogue: The Shadow Yet to Come

*A glimpse of the future—some time after Asteron was refounded.*

The shadow crossed Asteron before the sound of its engines arrived.

People looked up to see a Skycarrier moving above the clouds, physically larger than Vhargos had been. It did not turn or deploy troops. On Alpha’s maps, Asteron remained little more than ruins beside an old road.

Its target lay across the strait: Auregrad.

Hundreds of Auregrad Wyvern Corps soldiers rose from the coastal fortresses. Their formation filled the sky, yet from a distance they looked like birds before the flying vessel.

Alpha soldiers and their allies stood on its decks. Wind–Lightning engines turned without rest. In the holds waited Delta Titans, saurian giants, and Behemoths.

Their order was simple: break Auregrad, then move on the Great Tree—the greatest known Aethra nexus on the continent.

A Wyvern captain turned his mount.

“Warn every kingdom. This is not a scouting fleet.”

Far inside the Great Forest, another threat moved without such a vast shadow.

An Elf patrol found trees coated in black fiber. Before they could withdraw, emblem-less Hiveborn emerged from the roots. Two Elves were pulled into cocoons, their bodies drained for nutrients and Aethra by a Rogue Hive growing in secret.

One Elf escaped. Another held the passage long enough for the warning to reach Sylvaris.

In the north, Queen Zyra—Hive Archon of the Zyrath Hive—raised her head when a note of the Chorus was severed. She could not see the culprit, but she recognized the hunger left behind.

**Zavor the Severed.**

In Asteron, Zerak woke that same night, his hand gripping his spear before he was fully aware.

He heard no words.

Only hunger from the direction of the Great Forest—and silence where a voice should have been.

Outside his window, the Skycarrier’s shadow was already gone.

Two threats moved along the same timeline.

Asteron was not yet the target of either.

Yet.

**End of Volume One**
