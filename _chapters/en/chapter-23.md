# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 23: The Enemy Who Did Not Come

Reika’s forces gathered on the southern ridge. Asteron prepared for a siege.

At sunrise, Reika came alone.

“I ordered them to stop. Some obeyed. Others went to Gorzan.”

Serena opened the gate only wide enough for one person. “Why should we trust you?”

“You should not.” Reika offered her sword hilt-first. “But an invasion would bring more than one blade.”

Luca took the weapon, then returned it.

Serena stared at him.

“If she planned to stab me, returning it may have been a poor decision.”

“That is not a defense.”

“I know.”

Reika named the groups still bound to Gorzan: the Oni Magus, two surviving warriors of the Beta War, and several ogre companies. The orcs had scattered decades ago and refused the old banner.

“Without them, the Beta Sigil will not recognize me,” Reika said.

“Do you require it to lead?” Zerak asked.

“My people require proof.”

“Proof may follow a choice. It does not always precede it.”

That day, the army expected to attack instead helped clear the southern road. Suspicion did not vanish, but no blade was drawn.

The enemy who did not come gave Asteron one peaceful day.

Gorzan used it to open a sealed chamber that should never have been touched.

**End of Chapter 23**
