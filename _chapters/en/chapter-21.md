# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 21: Blood on Stone

The attack came while workers repaired the southern gate.

Only twelve Beta raiders advanced, ordered to damage the Core channels. Serena struck her shield with her sword.

“Here!”

Earth strengthened the ground beneath her. Their first charge broke against the shield, buying Open Road time.

Lysara’s Water Whip extinguished their torches. Pip shot through a shield strap and slipped beneath a swing with his dagger. Zerak used twin Dark Lances to weaken two fighters without striking vital organs.

Luca held his Fireball when he saw how young one raider was.

“Drop your weapon!”

The youth attacked. Luca parried and knocked him down with his shoulder—then cried out when a hilt struck his ribs.

“Aargh! Yes, that is still part of my body!”

Reika arrived and cut off the raiders’ retreat with a line of flame.

“Who gave the order?”

Their leader tried to bite poison, but Pip struck his jaw with a dagger hilt.

“No. We worked hard to catch you.”

They confessed that Gorzan had ordered the raid without Reika’s knowledge.

Reika tore the Beta emblem from their leader’s shoulder. “You wore my father’s banner to attack people I had not named enemies.”

“Gorzan decided for you.”

Her eyes burned. “That was his mistake.”

Blood had been spilled, but no one died. To Luca, the victory felt fragile. To Gorzan, it was humiliation.

**End of Chapter 21**
