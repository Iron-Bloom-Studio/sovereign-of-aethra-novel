# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 36: Leviathan Covenant

The Core plans revealed one final function: every city channel could be woven into an Aethra spear, designed to kill a Leviathan or force a Covenant.

“Vhargos will not recognize us,” Reika said.

“And we have no right to force it into our service,” Luca replied. “We stop the attack. Nothing more.”

They prepared the greatest Weave they had attempted.

Serena anchored the Core. Lysara directed Wind and Water through cooling lines. Zerak absorbed overflow. Pip ran between valves, fixing manual locks. Reika and Kaien supplied Fire from the south. Luca stood at the nexus.

Six affinities did not become a seventh. Each remained distinct while moving toward one purpose.

Luca saw every thread—and every limit.

“Serena, reduce by half.”

“I can still—”

“The stone beneath you is cracking.”

She obeyed.

“Zerak, release overflow east.”

“That will destroy a tower.”

“The tower is empty.”

Pip shouted from a gear wheel, “Valve five is stuck!”

“Use the persuasive method!”

His dagger struck the handle. “You finally learned!”

The Core lit in full. A six-colored spear formed over Asteron.

Luca did not feel like a king.

He felt like a knot that could not be allowed to break.

**End of Chapter 36**
