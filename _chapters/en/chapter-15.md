# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 15: The Heart Beneath Stone

The Asteron Core had not failed from age.

Lysara found tool marks on its regulator rings. Pip found new bolts among the ancient metal. Someone had attempted to dismantle it only months ago.

“The relic theft was not the beginning,” Serena said.

Zerak studied a black fracture. “Pressure is rising from below. If we activate the Core, it will rise with the flow.”

“Then we do not activate it,” Luca said.

Lysara looked mildly surprised.

“I can learn.”

They searched the service rooms. Pip recovered maintenance plans. Serena cleared collapsed passages. Zerak absorbed clotted Dark Aethra from a valve. Lysara ran Water through the pipes to locate leaks.

In the final room they found three looters. Two were dead. The last, Marrek, lay trapped beneath a beam.

“He abandoned his partners,” Pip said.

“And we are not him,” Luca replied.

Serena lifted the beam with Earth-reinforced footing. Luca pulled Marrek free and used Heal.

Marrek confessed that a masked patron had paid them to remove old keys and open Asteron’s flow. The patron wore a ring marked by a divided sun.

Before losing consciousness, he pointed south. “The thing below woke when we turned the Core.”

A Ruin Golem rose from the floor.

Serena took its first blow, shield shuddering. Zerak weakened its center. Lysara targeted the right joint. Pip searched for the mechanical lock.

“Luca?” Pip called.

“I will do the less intelligent part.”

Luca struck its chest with Fireball and drew its attention.

“Yes,” Pip shouted, “that is your part!”

Working in turns, they disabled the golem without destroying the Core.

When the dust settled, Serena studied the dead channels. “We cannot leave this.”

“We also cannot repair it with five people in one night,” Luca said.

For the first time, the next decision was larger than a quest.

They had to decide whether Asteron was worth guarding.

**End of Chapter 15**
