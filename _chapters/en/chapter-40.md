# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 40: Asteron Breathes Again

Gorzan was buried near Raizen, but not beneath his shadow.

Reika chose the stone:

**GORZAN—HE GUARDED, HE LOST HIS WAY, HE RETURNED.**

Kaien, Hagan, and Moro recognized Reika as leader. The Beta Sigil remained incomplete. She did not force it.

“I will build a unity that can refuse me. If the Sigil never chooses me, I must still be worthy without it.”

Asteron repaired the damage. The Core now sent part of its flow south under shared oversight. It could not heal all Beta land, but it proved life did not have to be stolen.

Open Road returned to the square with wounds, ruined equipment, and Serena’s unsalvageable shield.

Pip placed its halves on a table. “We could sell this as an artifact.”

“No,” Serena said.

“Two artifacts?”

“Pip.”

Luca laughed, then held his ribs. “Ouch. Apparently laughter still has a price.”

Lysara recorded the nature of Sovereign Weave: not Bestowal, not copied affinity, but the weaving of strength consciously offered. Much remained unknown.

On the day of proclamation, the gate received an official stone:

**25 STONEWARD 1063 TC**  
**ASTERON REFOUNDED**  
**43 YEARS AFTER THE AETHRA SCAR, 12 LONGNIGHT 1020 TC**

Luca stood with Serena, Lysara, Zerak, and Pip.

He had no crown. Asteron was not a kingdom. The hall still lacked a finished roof.

Yet when the lamps lit and the western road opened, the city drew its first long breath in forty-three years.

This time, it did not breathe alone.

**End of Chapter 40**
