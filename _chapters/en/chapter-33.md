# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 33: Those Who Turned

Ogres and oni still bound by Gorzan guarded the ritual chamber.

Hagan and Moro led the way. They had once fought beside Gorzan. Now they parried old comrades while calling each by name.

“Daro! Your child is safe!”

“Mei! There are no chains left!”

One by one, guards turned against the ritual nodes. Kaien contained Gorzan’s Fire, though his hands burned again.

At the final door, Reika faced Gorzan within a ring of magma.

“They will abandon you as they abandoned your father,” he said.

“No. Some may leave. And I will allow them to choose.”

“That is why you will never be strong enough.”

He shattered the final seal.

Reika charged too late.

Fire fell downward, as if the mountain inhaled. Something impossibly large moved below.

Gorzan laughed—until another voice laughed through him.

**End of Chapter 33**
