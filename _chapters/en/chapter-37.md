# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 37: The Fall of Vhargos

The Aethra spear flew.

Gorzan forced Vhargos to face it. Reika, Hagan, and Moro severed the final chains at the same moment.

Vhargos became free just as the spear pierced its fractured scales.

The explosion resembled a sun breaking without sound.

The Magma Whale split apart. Burning stones fell. Lysara and Serena formed a dome of Mist and Earth. Zerak nearly collapsed containing the last surge. Pip flew from a gear and landed on Luca.

“Ouch!”

“Alive?”

“For now. Please get off.”

In the sky, Vhargos dissolved into a red river of light.

Reika saw Gorzan fall and caught him at the edge of the chasm.

“It is over,” she said. “Come home.”

For an instant, he was only a tired old man.

Then red ash moved against the wind.

Vhargos’s Last Echo—Aethra persisting after the destruction of its body—flowed toward the one who had forced it.

“Move him!” Lysara screamed.

Too late.

Magma light entered through Gorzan’s eyes, mouth, and wounds.

Vhargos’s body had fallen.

Its will had not finished.

**End of Chapter 37**
