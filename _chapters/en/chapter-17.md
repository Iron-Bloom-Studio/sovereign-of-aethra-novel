# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 17: Asteron

They stayed for two weeks.

Two weeks became patched roofs, cleared roads, temporary fences, and a communal kitchen that never had enough salt. The first merchant arrived on the ninth day, complained continuously, and rented a warehouse before sunset.

Asteron was not yet a city. It was no longer only a ruin.

Serena organized guards without turning the settlement into a barracks. Lysara mapped the Core. Zerak patrolled at night until workers stopped reaching for weapons at the sight of him. Pip managed traps, stores, and prices that were “too human to be reasonable.”

Luca helped with everything and perfected nothing.

On the fourteenth afternoon they raised a new sign:

**ASTERON**

Luca refused to add “New.”

“If we call it New Asteron, the old city loses its name.”

“And using the same name means accepting its history,” Serena said.

“Yes.”

Pip stood on a ladder with nails in his mouth. The sign leaned badly.

“A little left,” Lysara said.

“My left or yours?”

“This is why I should hold the hammer,” Serena replied.

That night, the southern watch sounded an alarm.

Six horned figures stood on the road. Their leader was a young oni woman with dark red hair and a gaze steadier than any drawn blade. Behind her, two aging Oni Warriors carried a torn Beta banner.

She looked from Asteron’s lights to the great southern grave.

“Who gave you the right to build on my father’s tomb?”

Luca descended from the gate.

He did not know her name yet.

Gorzan, far to the south, called her Raizen’s daughter.

Her name was Reika.

**End of Chapter 17**
