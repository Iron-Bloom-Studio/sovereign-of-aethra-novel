# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 30: Fire from the South

The third day arrived with falling ash.

Armored Lizards, Ash Hounds, and red-cored golems fled the opening magma channels toward Asteron.

Serena struck her shield. “Here! If you want the walls, pass me first!”

Lysara formed Mist from the canals, cooling the hounds. Pip fired bolts wrapped in Luca’s flame—Fire Bolts—through gaps in lizard armor. Zerak planted Dark Lances in the ground, weakening golem legs for the guards.

The people fought beside them, carrying water, bolts, and the wounded.

Luca used Heal until his arms shook. He stopped before collapse, drank, and returned to the line. New power did not erase the limits of his body.

At sunset, the first wave broke.

Then the southern ground split.

A magma fin rose, taller than Asteron’s tower. It vanished again, but the heat painted the sky red.

“Vhargos,” Lysara whispered.

Not the full body.

Only the shadow of something rising.

**End of Chapter 30**
