# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 35: Leviathan Above Asteron

Vhargos’s shadow covered Asteron.

The civilians sheltered in Core tunnels. Serena stood at the gate, tiny beneath a creature the size of a hill.

“Do not stop all of it,” Luca warned.

“I know.”

A fin descended. Serena raised three Earth walls in sequence. The first shattered. The second melted. The third deflected the strike away from the tunnels.

Lysara pulled Water from the canals into cooling mist. Pip fired tethered bolts into a cracked stone scale. Zerak struck that mark with Dark Lance, weakening the Aethra beneath it.

“Now!”

Luca’s Fireball entered the fracture. It was small beside Vhargos, but their combined attack broke a single immense scale.

Vhargos roared.

Gorzan pulled its chain. Reika leapt onto a ruined tower and cut one link with her burning sword.

The Leviathan threw her into open air. Luca caught her hand, nearly losing his shoulder.

“Aargh! You are much heavier while falling!”

“Then let go!”

“Not helpful!”

Serena pulled them both up.

They had wounded Vhargos, but the fractures released even greater heat. To defeat it, they needed Asteron’s Core—and had to ensure it never again became one person’s weapon.

**End of Chapter 35**
