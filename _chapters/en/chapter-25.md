# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 25: Raizen's Legacy

The archives named the creature Vhargos: the Magma Whale, a low-class Leviathan once sealed beneath the south. Low-class did not mean weak. It was only a comparison to beings capable of swallowing fleets or changing seasons.

Vhargos had never recognized a master.

“Gorzan needs a Covenant, a Sigil, and the creature’s recognition,” Lysara said. “He has none.”

“He has anger,” Pip replied.

“That is not a ritual component.”

“People often treat it like one.”

Reika admitted that Raizen had refused to awaken Vhargos. He sought Asteron’s Core and the Beta Sigil because their power could serve his people. Gorzan was turning that legacy into permission for the very act Raizen avoided.

In the sealed chamber, a relief showed Raizen before a burning whale, his sword lowered.

“He said power that does not choose you will consume you,” Reika whispered.

“Then we show Gorzan,” Luca said.

“He will call it a human forgery.”

Luca clenched his hand. “So anything that disagrees with him is false?”

“You sound angry,” Serena noted.

“I am angry.”

“Good. At least you are not denying the facts.”

As they left, the earth trembled. A song moved through stone like a whale calling beneath an ocean.

Vhargos was not awake.

But it had heard someone calling.
