# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 26: The Sigil That Would Not Answer

Reika called the people who still followed her.

“I do not ask you to choose Raizen’s blood,” she said. “Choose what we build after him.”

The Oni Warriors remained silent. The Magus watched the south. Several ogres pledged themselves, but no orc representative came.

The Sigil fragment warmed, then went dark.

“Failed,” someone whispered.

“Incomplete,” Lysara corrected.

Reika lowered her head. “The result is the same.”

Luca sat beside her. “I thought Sovereign power would answer every question.”

“You do not even know what Sovereign means.”

“Exactly. Very unhelpful.”

He showed his palm. “I do not make people obey. I can only weave power they choose to give.”

“And if they refuse?”

“Then you do not get to use their strength yet.”

That night, an orc messenger arrived only to say they would follow neither Gorzan nor Reika. They would not become a condition for opening the old Sigil again.

Reika accepted their refusal.

For the first time, the fragment gave a faint glow.

“Respected choice leaves a mark,” Zerak said.

South of them, Gorzan chose the opposite. He bound the Oni Magus and veterans to his ritual and fed Fire into Vhargos’s seal.

**End of Chapter 26**
