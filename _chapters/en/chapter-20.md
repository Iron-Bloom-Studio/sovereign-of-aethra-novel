# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 20: The Demon Lord's Daughter

Reika camped outside the walls. She refused Luca’s room; Serena refused to admit her soldiers. Somehow the balance held.

For two days they searched the battlefield. Beneath an empty grave, Pip found a fragment of the Beta Sigil.

It remained cold in Reika’s hand.

“I am Raizen’s daughter.”

“The Sigil may not care about blood,” Lysara said. “It may answer recognition.”

An Oni Warrior bowed to Reika. Nothing changed.

A red spear of fire struck from the hill. Reika turned and swept it aside with her flame-coated sword.

An Oni Magus descended. “Gorzan orders the humans away from Raizen’s grave.”

“I did not order an attack.”

“Gorzan is Beta’s acting king.”

“And I am still Raizen’s daughter.”

Luca saw that Reika faced more than Asteron. Her people respected her name, but habit still bound them to Gorzan.

That night she sat near Asteron’s fire—far enough away to deny that it was a visit.

“My father wanted our people to live,” she said.

“Then he made others fear they would not,” Luca replied.

“If I choose another path, some will call me weak.”

“Some people call me foolish almost every day.”

Pip raised a hand. “I do not say almost.”

Reika gave a small snort. It was not quite laughter, but it made both Oni Warriors glance at each other.

At dawn she withdrew her ultimatum.

Gorzan did not.

**End of Chapter 20**
