# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 18: Those Who Returned

Reika did not attack. That unsettled Asteron’s guards more than an open threat.

She demanded access to Raizen’s grave and refused to disarm. Serena refused to open the gate to an armed company.

Pip whispered, “If this lasts longer, I am selling chairs.”

Luca offered a compromise: Reika could enter with one guard, escorted by Luca, Serena, and Zerak.

Inside, Reika saw humans, a Goblin, and dwarves repairing the same channel.

“Beta once promised a place for those rejected by other kingdoms,” she said.

“Then war came,” Serena replied.

“I know.”

At the southern grave, Reika knelt before an unnamed stone. Her hand trembled as an Oni Warrior laid down a fragment of old banner.

“My father died here.”

“Yes,” Luca said.

“And you relit his city.”

“Yes.”

“Then you awakened the reason he came.”

She gave Luca three days to abandon Asteron. He promised to spend them discovering the truth about Raizen.

“Truth does not always save people,” Reika said.

“No. But lies nearly always wound someone else.”

An Oni Magus watched through a flame and carried the news south to Gorzan.

**End of Chapter 18**
