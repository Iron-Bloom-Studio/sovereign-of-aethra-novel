# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 34: The Leviathan's Call

Vhargos rose as a colossal whale of magma and black stone. It swam through the low sky, each sweep of its fins collapsing cliffs.

There was no Covenant. No recognition.

Gorzan drove fire chains into its body and ordered it toward Asteron. Vhargos obeyed once, then pulled back hard enough to break his shoulder.

“He does not control it,” Lysara said. “He only made it angry in our direction.”

Open Road withdrew with the army. Serena raised Earth walls against falling stone. Lysara redirected ash with Wind. Zerak absorbed Dark Aethra from the fractures. Pip marked safe paths with small flares.

Luca sensed the link between Gorzan and Vhargos. It was not a bond. It was a wound forced to remain open.

“I may be able to break the chains.”

“Then Vhargos is free,” Serena said.

“If we do not, Gorzan points it at the city.”

Reika looked up. “We break the chain, then bring Vhargos down.”

Pip swallowed. “A very simple plan for something the size of the sky.”

Behind them, the Magma Whale sang and the clouds began to burn.

**End of Chapter 34**
