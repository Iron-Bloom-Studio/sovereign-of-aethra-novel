# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 38: Binding Reversal

Gorzan rose with movements that were not his.

His horns lengthened into black stone. Magma fractures crossed his body. Human and Leviathan voices overlapped.

Lysara named it **Binding Reversal**. The forced bond had inverted after Vhargos’s death. The controller became the vessel, and the Last Echo used the path Gorzan had created.

Reika refused to raise her sword.

“Gorzan, hear me.”

The creature struck her through a wall.

“He is still inside,” she said as she rose.

“Yes,” Zerak replied. “And something is consuming him.”

They had to sever every Aethra chain within Gorzan. Zerak weakened the nodes with Dark. Lysara cooled the fractures. Serena held his attacks with a broken shield, sword, and low Earth walls.

Pip climbed Gorzan’s blind side and drove two daggers through the binding bracers.

“Now, Luca!”

Luca reached into the Weave. Vhargos’s fire burned his hand and mind. For one terrible moment, he nearly drew all of it into himself.

That would only repeat Gorzan’s mistake.

He did not take the power. He opened a path into the emptied Core.

Gorzan and Vhargos screamed together.

One node remained, buried in the heart.

**End of Chapter 38**
