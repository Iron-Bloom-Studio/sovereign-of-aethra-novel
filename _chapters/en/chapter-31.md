# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 31: Open Road Against Beta

Gorzan sent his final army to seize the Core before Vhargos fully awakened.

Open Road met them on the southern bridge. Reika walked at Asteron’s front.

“Gorzan gives you no choice,” Kaien shouted to his former comrades. “We do.”

Some oni lowered their weapons. Others attacked because their families remained hostage.

Serena held the line without killing. Lysara used Water to extinguish fire in the oath chains. Pip slipped beneath the bridge to cut their seals. Zerak faced an ogre captain, weakening every blow until the great hammer could no longer rise.

Luca and Reika combined their Fire into a curved wall that separated the soldiers from Gorzan.

“Dual Art: Ember Divide,” Lysara called.

“We are naming it now?” Luca shouted.

“Records require terms!”

When the hostages were freed, more of Gorzan’s army turned.

The bridge did not fill with bodies as it had during the Beta War. It filled with people lowering weapons and noticing their hands were shaking.

Gorzan watched from the ridge.

Losing his army did not make him surrender.

It convinced him that only Vhargos remained loyal.

**End of Chapter 31**
