# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 28: Ultimatum for Asteron

Gorzan sent a black stone carrying his voice.

“Leave Asteron before the third sunrise, or Vhargos will cleanse Raizen’s grave in fire.”

Asteron’s small council divided. Merchants wished to flee. Larkhill’s families refused to lose another home. Serena prepared evacuation routes even as she strengthened the walls.

Luca promised no easy victory.

“Vhargos may be stronger than us. If you leave, we protect the road. If you remain, know the risk.”

A mason asked, “And you?”

“I stay.”

Pip raised a hand. “We know. He is terrible at leaving danger.”

Some civilians departed with children and patients. Others remained. No one was shamed for choosing safety.

Reika offered to surrender herself.

“He does not want you,” Zerak said. “He wants your choice to justify his own.”

Reika tore the ultimatum apart.

Asteron answered by lighting every lamp in its restored district.

From the south, Gorzan saw the light and began the final countdown.

**End of Chapter 28**
