# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 19: The Grave in the South

Asteron’s war records were incomplete. Their final pages had been burned.

Pip opened a hidden archive beneath the hall, complaining that ancient people “liked doors that believed they were riddles.”

Inside, Lysara found the old king’s reports. Beta’s land had been starving as southern Aethra flows weakened. Raizen asked to use Asteron’s network to restore them. Asteron refused, fearing he would seize control.

Raizen then conquered unrelated orc clans, seeking to open the Beta Sigil—a symbol that required recognition from several peoples.

“He was not a misunderstood hero,” Serena said.

“Nor a monster without a reason,” Luca replied.

“Purpose does not erase choice,” Zerak said.

They showed Reika the reports. She read until the paper crumpled in her hand.

“Gorzan said humans poisoned our land.”

“Partly true,” Lysara said gently. “Asteron’s use of Aethra changed the flow. But Raizen also forced the orcs to kneel.”

Reika nearly threw the pages away. She stopped herself.

“I do not accept your city,” she said. “But I will hear the rest.”

Their first agreement was not peace.

It was one night without an attack.

**End of Chapter 19**
