# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 27: Those Forced to Kneel

The Oni Magus, Kaien, reached Asteron with burns across both hands.

“Gorzan bound our oaths to the ritual. If we leave, the fire consumes our clans.”

Reika wanted to attack at once. Serena stopped her.

“Enter without a plan and he gains more people to bind.”

Open Road prepared a rescue. Lysara mapped the circle. Pip slipped inside to mark the oath chains. Zerak would absorb the curse as it broke. Serena would hold the exit. Luca and Reika would face the guards without killing them.

Pip crawled through an old lava channel and found the veterans Hagan and Moro chained to pillars by fire.

“They said you volunteered,” he whispered.

“We volunteered so our families would not burn,” Hagan growled.

“Humans also have stupid words for coercion.”

Smoke bombs covered the room. Serena held the ogres. Lysara drove a Gust through Pip’s stone fragments, forming Stone Javelins that severed the chain hooks.

Zerak contained the surge. His carapace cracked.

“Now!”

Luca used Sovereign Weave. He did not take the oath fire. He connected Serena’s Earth, Lysara’s Wind, Zerak’s Dark, and Reika’s flame so the curse lost its path to the hostages.

The chains broke.

Hagan and Moro stood before Reika.

“We have not chosen your war,” Moro said. “But we no longer fight because we fear Gorzan.”

It was not loyalty.

It was the beginning of an army that chose to stand.

**End of Chapter 27**
