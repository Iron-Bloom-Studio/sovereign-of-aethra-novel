# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 32: The Voice of Raizen's Daughter

Gorzan sealed himself inside the ritual chamber with Vhargos’s core.

Reika stood before every gathered soldier without crown or Sigil.

“My father wanted to save Beta. He also conquered those who did not choose him. I can love him without repeating his wrongs.”

Some oni wept in silence.

“Gorzan guarded us for forty-three years. Today he asks us to die so his past can remain correct. I will not call that loyalty.”

She turned toward Asteron’s humans.

“This city severed its Core and let our land suffer. They are not innocent. Neither are we. That is why no future can belong to one side claiming purity.”

Luca did not add a speech. These words belonged to Reika.

The Sigil fragment rose. Light filled almost all of it, stopping at one missing section.

The orcs had not recognized her. The Sigil remained incomplete.

Still, Beta’s soldiers knelt to Reika by choice.

Kaien called her leader.

Not queen. Not yet.

Below, Gorzan roared that they were traitors.

Vhargos answered louder.

**End of Chapter 32**
