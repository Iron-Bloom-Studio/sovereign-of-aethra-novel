# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 14: The City That Waited

Asteron was silent, but not empty.

Armored Lizards rested among the ruins. Birds nested in roofless towers. Great roots split the main road and lifted stones like frozen waves.

Serena led with her shield. Pip marked safe paths with chalk. Lysara copied inscriptions. Zerak watched dark windows.

In the square they found the remains of the old battle: human bones, a broken oni horn, rusted orc weapons, and fragments of Auregrad armor.

“Beta War,” Serena said. “The allied armies stopped Raizen here.”

The relic led them to a round building at the city’s center. Six hollows ringed its bronze door.

“Six affinities,” Lysara said. “One balancing mechanism.”

“Pip has no affinity,” Luca noted.

Pip lifted his dagger. “I have a persuasive opening tool.”

“Do not stab the ancient door,” Serena and Lysara said together.

They fed it Fire, Light, Earth, Wind, Water, and Dark. Every hollow lit, but the door remained closed.

Pip peered through a gap. “You lit everything. This gear still does not move.”

He cleaned sand from the mechanism with wire and pressed a physical catch.

The door opened.

Below waited a vast chamber. At its center stood a structure of stone and metal as large as a house, covered in black fractures. Light spread across the walls, revealing a map of Aethra channels throughout the city.

Most were dead.

One continued pulsing south.

Beneath the map were two words:

**ASTERON CORE.**

**End of Chapter 14**
