# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 39: Fire No Body Could Bear

The final node could not be removed without destroying Gorzan’s body.

Reika understood from Lysara’s face.

“No.”

Gorzan surfaced behind the magma eyes. “Reika.”

At last, he heard her.

“I only wanted them not to die again.”

“I know.”

“I failed Raizen.”

“No. You loved him too long in the shape of anger.”

Vhargos seized him again. Serena was thrown down. Zerak lost his spear. Pip had no bolts left. Lysara could barely stand.

Luca gave them the only choice: sever the node together and drain the Last Echo into the Core. Gorzan would not survive.

When himself again for a single breath, Gorzan nodded.

Reika wept without sound, then raised her blade.

Serena anchored Earth. Lysara guided the flow. Zerak weakened Vhargos’s will. Pip broke the final bracer. Reika cut the node. Luca wove the way out.

Magma light left Gorzan and entered the Core, divided into harmless heat across empty channels.

Gorzan died in Reika’s arms.

Not as a king.

As an old man who finally released a war he had carried for forty-three years.

**End of Chapter 39**
