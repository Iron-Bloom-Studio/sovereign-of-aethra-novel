# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 22: The Truth Left Behind

Lysara arranged war reports, Core records, and Reika’s testimony into a single history.

Raizen came because Beta’s land was starving. Asteron refused him out of fear. He pursued the Beta Sigil by conquering peoples who had never chosen him. War grew from fears that fed each other.

“People will hate this answer,” Luca said.

“Truth is not required to be comfortable,” Lysara replied.

The final commander’s record revealed that Asteron deliberately severed the Core during the last battle. It stopped Raizen, but worsened Beta’s dying land for decades.

“Auregrad calls it a necessary sacrifice,” Serena said.

“Necessary for whom?” Reika asked.

Serena did not hide from the question. “That is what we must admit.”

Luca proposed opening one small flow south once the Core was stable, controlled by several anchors so neither Asteron nor Beta could command it alone.

“So no single ruler can turn it,” Reika said.

“Including Luca,” Pip added.

“That warning arrived quickly.”

“Prevention.”

They opened a test channel. The Sigil fragment remained dark, but dry earth around Raizen’s grave became damp.

Proof spoke more clearly than any speech.

Gorzan heard that Asteron was giving water to Beta soil.

He did not see peace.

He saw the theft of Raizen’s legacy.

**End of Chapter 22**
