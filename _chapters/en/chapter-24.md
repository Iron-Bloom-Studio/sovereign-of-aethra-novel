# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 24: The Acting King

Gorzan came to Raizen’s grave at night with the Oni Magus and two veterans.

He was broad, gray at the temples, and fire shone through cracks in his stone bracers. He looked at Reika like a disappointed grandfather, then at Luca like a thief.

“I watched Raizen die here,” he said. “Now humans use his daughter to open the same Core.”

“I came by choice,” Reika replied.

“A choice shaped by their lies.”

Luca offered the records. Gorzan burned them unread.

Luca’s anger rose. “If you reject evidence before seeing it, do not pretend you seek truth.”

Serena immediately moved in front of him.

Gorzan declared himself Beta’s Acting King until the Sigil chose an heir. The veterans knelt out of habit and debt, not love.

Before leaving, he revealed a black-red fragment.

“Raizen sought power to save our people. I will finish his work.”

Lysara paled. “That belongs to a Leviathan seal.”

Gorzan’s smile held no joy. “If Asteron has a heart, Beta will have a god.”

“Gorzan, do not,” Reika said.

For the first time, he did not look back when she called his name.

**End of Chapter 24**
