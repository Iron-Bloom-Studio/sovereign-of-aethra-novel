# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 13: The Road to Asteron

They bound Calder, treated Venn, and returned for Harlan and the injured donkey. Serena sent the wounded men toward the Guild post with food, water, and a message for Arklune.

“You are letting two thieves go?” Calder asked.

“We are letting two wounded people live,” Luca replied. “There is a difference.”

Open Road followed the relic southwest. Buried paving stones emerged beneath the brush, along with broken pillars bearing Asteron’s crest.

They reached abandoned Larkhill near sunset. Its well remained clear and an apple orchard had grown wild.

Pip opened a storehouse, then closed it immediately.

“Monster?” Luca asked.

“Spiders.”

“Large ones?”

“No. Many normal ones. Worse.”

They camped in a stone house. Lysara explained that the old towers had once regulated Aethra flowing toward Asteron. A single damaged regulator had displaced monsters across an entire valley.

“What can the center do?” Serena asked.

“That,” Lysara said, “is what we must learn.”

Before dawn, the mark woke Luca. A thin road of light seemed to move beneath the earth. Zerak was already standing by the well.

“You cannot sleep either?” Luca asked.

“I require less than humans.”

“That is not a comforting answer.”

“It was not intended to be.”

At sunrise, the land opened into a wide plain. Broken walls rose through the mist, followed by roofless towers, stone bridges, and buildings split by roots.

Asteron had not sunk underground.

It stood beneath the open sky, as though the world had spent forty-three years choosing not to see it.

At the gate, an inscription survived:

**ASTERON GUARDS THE WESTERN ROAD.**

Luca touched the stone.

Far inside the city, something beat once.

**End of Chapter 13**
