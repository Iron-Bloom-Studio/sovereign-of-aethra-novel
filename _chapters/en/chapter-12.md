# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 12: The Tower's Call

The fourth toll never came. The first Dire Wolf charged from the grass.

“Serena!”

Her shield met it with a crash. Luca sent a low Fireball across the ground, splitting the pack without igniting the dry field.

“Inside the tower!” Lysara called.

Pip slipped through first. His bolt struck a rope supporting a cracked crate. Calder knelt before the pale stone relic, now fixed inside an ancient metal cradle. Venn lay near the stairs, still breathing.

“I was paid to open it!” Calder shouted.

“That thing is calling every monster in the valley,” Luca said. “Your work has failed impressively.”

Calder pulled a lever.

A bell that did not exist struck through their bones. Two small Stone Golems rose from the floor. Serena stopped one. Zerak drove Dark Lance through the other’s knee joint, weakening the stone long enough for it to stumble.

“The relic is not the source,” Lysara said. “It is a key being forced the wrong way.”

Pip threw a smoke bomb at Calder. “Then take the key!”

Luca burst through the smoke, parried Calder’s knife, and struck his wrist with the sword hilt. The relic came free. Cracks raced across the floor.

Luca caught the stone.

The mark in his palm flared. He sensed several currents colliding—earth, wind, fire, darkness, and the small light within himself.

“Do not fight it alone,” Serena said, anchoring the floor with Earth.

Lysara redirected the wild flow with a narrow Gust. Zerak absorbed the surge threatening Venn. Pip climbed the cradle and forced a jammed metal ring around with his dagger.

“Now, Luca!”

Luca did not command their power. He found the place where each current could meet.

Light wound five strands into a single quiet pulse. The toll ceased. Outside, the monsters lost their purpose and withdrew.

A pebble fell beside Pip.

“I vote we leave before the building changes its mind.”

Beneath the cradle, Lysara found a road engraved into the stone. It led southwest to one surviving name:

**ASTERON.**

A line of light pointed toward it.

**End of Chapter 12**
