# Sovereign of Aethra

## Chapter 16: Five Threads

They returned to Arklune with Marrek, their notes, and copies of Asteron’s maps.

The Guild Master disliked the report. Merchants disliked it more. A reopened southwestern road meant opportunity, monsters, looters, and territorial disputes.

“We need people to stop the Core from exploding,” Luca said.

“That sounds worse.”

“It is.”

Eldren allowed Lysara to continue her research, but forbade full activation without measurements. Auregrad authorized Serena to protect the civilian road.

“So you are staying?” Luca asked.

“My duty now runs in the same direction as yours.”

Pip grinned. “Soldier language for yes.”

They returned with healers, workers, and several Larkhill families. Open Road was not an army. They were simply the first people willing to stand there.

Lysara designed a gradual stabilization. Serena anchored Earth. Zerak absorbed overflow. Luca linked the currents. Pip operated the valves.

The first attempt threw Luca backward.

“Aargh!”

Serena caught him by the shoulder—on the side of his damaged ribs.

“Ouch, ouch—the other side!”

She released him. “Sorry.”

Pip watched Luca wince. “The great Sovereign, defeated by assistance.”

“No one calls me Sovereign.”

“Good. It is too long.”

On the second attempt, they did not force the Core awake. They wound five small currents around its fractures, leaving each power room to remain itself.

Luca sensed not more power, but connection. He knew when Serena held too hard, when Lysara’s wind drifted, and when Zerak accepted too much.

“Pip, third valve.”

“Already done.”

“Not yet.”

Pip struck the handle with his dagger. “Now it is.”

Light entered one district. A well sounded, water filled a channel, and old Aethra lamps lit one after another.

Not all of Asteron.

Only a beginning.

Luca understood that this power was not born from standing above his companions. It existed because he gave them room to be themselves.

**End of Chapter 16**
